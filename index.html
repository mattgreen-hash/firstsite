Welcome to Kingdom Prep

Mission: Building a brotherhood in Christ for lives of purpose.

Kingdom Prep Lutheran High School exists to provide young men with a rigorous, Christ-centered education, developing their God-given gifts to lead in the home, serve in the Church, engage in meaningful work, and transform the community.

Excellence

High standards in academics, character, and leadership.
